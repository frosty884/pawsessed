find the party

Golf Villa D, 4th floor

The Golf Villas sit along the golf course, a short walk from the main hotel. Building D is the last one, next to the little white Chapel on the Green.

  1. 1
    Start at the Hotel Entrance & Guest Registration (the main lobby by the big K&G Kilbourn City Grill sign) and walk out the front doors to the road.
  2. 2
    Go left at the road, away from the waterparks, along the big parking lot, then follow it as it bends down toward the golf course. Keep the villas on your right and pass the first Golf Villas building.
  3. 3
    Keep going to building D, the last villa at the end of the road, by the Chapel on the Green. Up to the 4th floor, Room 7416. Follow the bass 🔊
🗺️ Open in Google Maps

Driving in? Park in the villa lot right outside building D. Lost? Ask in the party chat.
